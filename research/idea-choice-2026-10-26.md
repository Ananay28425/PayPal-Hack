# PayGuard versus Assembly: decision on October 26, 2026

Recommendation: choose Assembly as the lead product for the two-person hackathon team. Retain necessary payment approval, authority checks and recovery as implementation requirements. This is a qualitative recommendation based on the current proposals, not a guarantee of any award or global originality.

## Decision distinctions

- Best overall build choice: Assembly, because the requested outcome, incompatible configuration, repair and PayPal purchase can form one understandable demonstration.
- Strongest credible, testable merchant-impact case: Assembly, conditional on representative configurations and user validation.
- More direct relevance to PayPal payment-risk infrastructure: PayGuard. Its theoretical platform value is substantial, but the proposal has no measured addressable share of the reported transaction losses.
- Most plausible initial adoption proposal: a narrow Assembly merchant/partner pilot. Global deployment is not established for either concept. PayGuard could also be piloted outside core payments, but platform-wide loss claims require additional evidence and internal access.
- Stronger current first-prize case: Assembly. Neither concept ensures first prize. The participant forecast does not supply a win-probability denominator.

## Evidence

PayPal reports $1.337 billion transaction losses in 2025 versus $1.114 billion in 2024. The table does not identify losses attributable to AI agents or avoidable by the proposed PayGuard controls. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1633917/000163391726000024/R133.htm

PayPal AP2 documents verifiable intent and mandates. Public Warden and AGENT GATE descriptions overlap strongly with the proposed policy/approval/execution/recovery flow. Public descriptions were refreshed, not implementations tested; no inference about these projects' eligibility for this event is made. https://developer.paypal.com/community/blog/PayPal-Agent-Payments-Protocol/ ; https://devpost.com/software/warden-a-paypal-firewall-for-ai-agents ; https://devpost.com/software/agent-gate-human-controlled-ai-payments

Assembly is not an established globally novel invention. Research on language models plus symbolic configuration predates this proposal. Its proposed distinction is the customer outcome, source-backed facts and unknowns, incremental repair, exact approval binding and complete purchase flow. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29947

The Hugging Face connector returned UNAVAILABLE on dataset and documentation calls. Official public guidance documentation retrieved via Exa supports grammar-constrained structured extraction, not correctness of extracted claims. Channel3 offers and variants provide a useful discovery input but do not guarantee complete critical compatibility fields or merchant checkout authority. https://huggingface.co/docs/text-generation-inference/en/conceptual/guidance ; https://docs.trychannel3.com/guides/response-overview

## Immediate validation gate

Within a bounded 48-hour feasibility pass, establish a source-backed catalogue in one technical category; check held-out compatible, incompatible and unknown cases against explicit rules; execute an actual PayPal sandbox order/approval/capture and reconcile its resource state; and observe intended users completing the core task. These are proposed gates, not completed results.

Assembly loses its recommendation if critical specifications cannot be established, the product cannot outperform a simple catalogue/form baseline on meaningful cases, or the end-to-end payment demonstration cannot be made reliable. Do not compensate with additional languages, sponsor logos or unsupported savings claims.

Sponsor requirement remains all ten. Evaluate each integration by its contribution to the same workflow and maintain a meaningful implementation trail. The official judging rubric weights implementation, design, impact, innovation and presentation equally. https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/rules
